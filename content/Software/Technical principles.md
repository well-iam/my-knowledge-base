- Goodhart's law states that "==when a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure==." Coined by British economist [Charles Goodhart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Goodhart) in 1975, it means that once a metric is tied to a reward or penalty, people will manipulate the system to hit the number, ruining the metric's ability to track real progress.
- Tracer bullet: ==a thin, end-to-end implementation of a feature or system that connects all major components—such as the user interface, backend, and database—using real, production-grade code==. Popularized by the book _The Pragmatic Programmer_, the concept mimics military tracer ammunition, which glows in flight so a shooter can see where the shots land and adjust their aim.
