
The research statement is particularly important for Ph.D. applicants.

### Research Problem
Clearly identify the problem you want to investigate.

### Existing Research
Demonstrate that you understand the current research landscape.

### Research Gap
Explain what remains unresolved.

### Proposed Direction
Describe how you would approach the problem.

### Research Significance
Explain why solving the problem matters.

### MBZUAI Fit
Explain why MBZUAI is an appropriate environment for your proposed research.
Your research statement should connect logically with your previous academic and technical experience.
